🔑 The parallelogram of forces is a scale diagram used to find the single resultant force when two forces act at a point but not along the same line.

📖 How It Works

⚠️ You cannot simply add or subtract forces that act at an angle — you must use the parallelogram method (geometry, not arithmetic).
1
Choose a scale e.g. 1 cm = 100 N. Write it on your diagram.
2
Draw the two force vectors from the same origin Use a protractor for the angle between them. Draw arrows to scale.
3
Complete the parallelogram Draw two more lines parallel to each force vector to form a four-sided shape (a parallelogram).
4
Draw the diagonal From the origin to the opposite corner. This diagonal is the resultant force.
5
Measure the diagonal Use your ruler and scale to find the magnitude. Use a protractor to find the direction.
✏️ Exam Tip

You will need: a protractor, a ruler, a sharp pencil, and a blank sheet of paper. Always state your scale clearly on the diagram.

📋 Key Points to Remember

📐 What it IS

A scale diagram of two force vectors drawn as adjacent sides of a parallelogram.

🎯 The Resultant

The diagonal of the parallelogram, starting from the origin of the two forces — that's your resultant.

📏 Angles Matter

The angle between the two adjacent sides of the parallelogram equals the angle between the two forces.

⚠️ Not Arithmetic!

You cannot add force magnitudes unless they act along the same line. Always use geometry for angled forces.

🔬 Investigating It

Use weights and pulleys. Three strings meet at a point; the resultant of two outer weights equals the middle weight (opposite direction).

⚖️ Same Line vs At an Angle

➡️ Forces Along Same Line

Add or subtract magnitudes directly.

e.g. 5 N + 3 N = 8 N (same direction)
e.g. 5 N − 3 N = 2 N (opposite)

↗️ Forces at an Angle

Use the parallelogram of forces.

Draw to scale → measure diagonal → read off resultant magnitude and direction.

🔢 Worked Example

🚗 Tow Rope Problem

Setup: A tow rope is attached to a car at two points 0.80 m apart. Both sections of rope make a 30° angle with each other (15° each side). The pull on each attachment must not exceed 3000 N.

Find: The maximum tension T in the main tow rope.

Method: Draw the parallelogram for two 3000 N forces at 30° to each other (scale: 1 cm = 1000 N). The diagonal from the origin gives the resultant.

T = 5800 N
💡 The resultant (tow rope tension) is larger than either individual force — this is always true when two forces act at a small angle to each other.

🧰 What You Need for the Diagram

📐 Protractor

To measure the angle between the two force vectors accurately.

📏 Ruler

To draw and measure the force vectors and the diagonal to scale.

✏️ Sharp Pencil

Precision is essential — a blunt pencil introduces errors in scale diagrams.

📄 Blank Paper

Plenty of space for a clear, large diagram with stated scale.

✍️ Exam Tips

✏️ Scale Diagrams

Always state your scale (e.g. "1 cm = 500 N") — marks are lost if you omit it. Choose a scale that fills most of the page.

✏️ Reading the Diagonal

Measure the diagonal carefully with a ruler, then convert back using your scale. Also measure its angle with a protractor to give the full vector answer.

🔔 Remember: the resultant is the diagonal that starts at the origin (where the two force arrows begin), NOT the other diagonal.
