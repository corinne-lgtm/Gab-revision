🧠 Big Ideas
🔄

Elastic Objects

Return to their original shape when deforming forces are removed (rubber bands, springs).

📦

Inelastic Objects

Do not return to their original shape — permanently deformed (e.g. polythene bag).

📐

Extension

The increase in length from the original length when a force is applied.

📈

Hooke's Law

Extension is directly proportional to force — as long as the limit of proportionality is not exceeded.

⚡ Hooke's Law Formula
Force = spring constant × extension
F = k × e
F = Force (newtons, N)
k = Spring constant (N/m)
e = Extension (metres, m)
The spring constant k equals the force needed per unit extension. A stiffer spring has a higher spring constant.
💡
Exam Tip — Finding k from a graph Plot Force (F) on the y-axis and extension (e) on the x-axis. The gradient of the straight line = spring constant k.
⚠️
Limit of Proportionality Once exceeded, extension is no longer proportional to force — the relationship becomes non-linear. Hooke's Law no longer applies.
📊 Graph Behaviour

Steel Spring

Straight line through origin — high limit of proportionality. Stays linear for much longer.

Rubber Band

Low limit of proportionality — quickly becomes non-linear after a small force.

Polythene Strip

Very low limit — extension is non-linear at low forces. Inelastic (doesn't recover).

Direct Proportion

Doubling the weight doubles the extension — only true before the limit of proportionality.
⚖️ Elastic vs Inelastic Deformation
✅ Elastic
  • Returns to original shape
  • Deformation is temporary
  • Example: spring, rubber band
  • Elastic potential energy stored
✗ Inelastic
  • Does not return to original shape
  • Deformation is permanent
  • Example: polythene bag
  • Energy is dissipated as heat

Exam Tip — Extension vs Total Length Extension = current length − original length. Don't confuse total length with extension — the question will usually give you the original length separately.

Exam Tip — Compression Hooke's Law also applies when a spring is compressed. In this case, the change in length is a compression, not an extension.
