Big picture: the story in 5 stages

1. Imperial Germany
Germany under Kaiser Wilhelm II, militarism, naval expansion and tension before 1914.
2. Weimar Germany
Defeat in WWI, Treaty of Versailles, democracy, instability and crisis.
3. Nazi rise to power
Depression, extremist support, Hitler becomes Chancellor, dictatorship begins.
4. Nazi Germany
Control, propaganda, police state, social policy, economy and persecution.
5. War and Holocaust
World War II, total war, Final Solution and Germany’s defeat in 1945.

Quick revision checks

Most important crisis?
1923 Ruhr + hyperinflation; 1929 Wall Street Crash and Depression.
Turning point for Hitler?
1933, when he becomes Chancellor and then starts removing opposition.
Most important control event?
1934 Night of the Long Knives removes SA threat and wins army support.
Most important persecution event?
1938 Kristallnacht and 1942 Wannsee Conference.

Remember this pattern

Imperial Germany → Weimar democracy → crises → Nazi rise → dictatorship → control and persecution → war and defeat.
