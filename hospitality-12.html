How hospitality and catering provisions operate

Learn → Quiz → Exam Practice. Your quiz score is saved on this device.

Lesson 1 of 5

Front of house (FOH)

Customer-facing roles include welcoming guests, taking bookings, serving food, handling payments and dealing with complaints. FOH gives customers their first impression.

Lesson 2 of 5

Back of house (BOH)

Kitchen and storage teams prepare food, manage deliveries, wash equipment, monitor temperatures and keep workplaces clean. Good communication between FOH and BOH prevents delays and errors.

Lesson 3 of 5

Equipment

Commercial kitchens use temperature probes, refrigerators, freezers, extraction systems, ovens, mixers and dishwashers. Equipment must be suitable, clean, maintained and used safely.

Lesson 4 of 5

Customer needs

Consider budget, age, service expectations, accessibility, dietary and religious requirements, allergies and intolerances. Ask and check rather than guess.

Lesson 5 of 5

Meeting specific requirements

Offer clear allergen information, safe preparation methods, accessible facilities and suitable menus. Check ingredients and cross-contact risks; staff should communicate accurate information.

Exam-style written questions

6 marks

Explain how front of house and back of house should work together for a customer with a food allergy.

Develop a clear process: FOH asks/checks the allergy and relays accurate details, BOH checks ingredients and cross-contact controls, FOH confirms only verified information and serves the correct dish.

Use these indicative points for self-assessment, not an automatic mark.

4 marks

Explain two ways a restaurant can meet different customer requirements.

Credit accessibility needs, suitable dietary choices, allergen information or adapted service, each linked to benefit.

Use these indicative points for self-assessment, not an automatic mark.
