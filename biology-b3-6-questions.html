1
Three types of enzymes found in the body are amylase, protease, and lipase. a) State where each enzyme is made in the body. b) State which reaction each enzyme catalyses. c) State where each enzyme works in the digestive system.
[9 marks]
2
Look at Figure 1 (from the revision page showing pH graphs for pepsin and pancreatic amylase). a) At which pH does pepsin work best? b) At which pH does pancreatic amylase work best? c) What happens to the activity of the enzymes as pH increases above the optimum? d) Explain why this change in activity happens.
[6 marks]
3
Explain the importance of the digestion of food in terms of the molecules involved and the role of enzymes in the gut.
[6 marks]
4

[3 marks]
5
Describe the role of bile in the digestion of fats. Where is bile produced and where is it stored?
[3 marks]
6

[2 marks]
7

[2 marks]

9

[3 marks]
