Graphs
Question 7[4 marks]

A line has equation 4x + 2y = 10.

(a) [2 marks]
Rearrange into the form y = mx + c, and state the gradient and y-intercept.
(b) [2 marks]
Determine whether the point (1, 3) lies on this line. Show your reasoning.

Mark Scheme — Q7 [4 marks]

  • [1] (a) Rearranges: y = 5 − 2x
  • [1] (a) Gradient = −2, y-intercept = 5
  • [1] (b) Substitutes x = 1: y = 5 − 2(1) = 3
  • [1] (b) Since this matches the given y-value, yes, (1,3) lies on the line
Ratio, Proportion & Rates of Change
Question 8[3 marks]

A recipe uses flour and sugar in the ratio 5 : 2. If 250 g of flour is used, how much sugar is needed?

Mark Scheme — Q8 [3 marks]

  • [1] Finds the scale factor: 250 ÷ 5 = 50
  • [1] Multiplies sugar ratio part by scale factor: 2 × 50
  • [1] = 100 g
Ratio, Proportion & Rates of Change Grade 7+
Question 9[3 marks]

y is inversely proportional to x. When x = 4, y = 9. Find y when x = 6.

Mark Scheme — Q9 [3 marks]

  • [1] y = k/x, so k = xy = 4 × 9 = 36
  • [1] y = 36/6
  • [1] = 6

Pythagoras & Trigonometry
Question 13[4 marks]

Use exact trigonometric values — no calculator needed.

(a) [2 marks]
A right-angled triangle has angle 30° and hypotenuse 8 cm. Find the length of the side opposite the 30° angle.
(b) [2 marks]
A right-angled isosceles triangle has two equal sides of length x and hypotenuse 7√2 cm. Find x. (Hint: the angles are 45°-45°-90°)

Mark Scheme — Q13 [4 marks]

  • [1] (a) opposite = 8 × sin30° = 8 × ½
  • [1] (a) = 4 cm
  • [1] (b) For a 45°-45°-90° triangle, hypotenuse = x√2
  • [1] (b) x√2 = 7√2 → x = 7 cm
Statistics & Probability
Question 14[3 marks]
(a) [2 marks]
Find the mean of: 4, 7, 9, 12, 13
(b) [1 mark]
A bag contains 5 red balls and 3 blue balls. Find the probability of picking a red ball.

Mark Scheme — Q14 [3 marks]

  • [1] (a) Sum = 4+7+9+12+13 = 45, divides by 5
  • [1] (a) Mean = 9
  • [1] (b) P(red) = 5 ÷ (5+3) = 5/8
Statistics & Probability Grade 7+
Question 15[3 marks]

A spinner has 4 equal sections numbered 1 to 4. It is spun twice. Find the probability that both spins land on an even number.

Mark Scheme — Q15 [3 marks]

  • [1] P(even on one spin) = 2/4 = 1/2
  • [1] Multiplies independent probabilities: 1/2 × 1/2
  • [1] = 1/4
