The Big Picture

🌈

White Light

Contains ALL colours of the visible spectrum — from violet to red.

🌡️

Infrared

📡

300 000 Hz → 3000 MHz. Used for radio, TV, Bluetooth, mobile phones.

Wave Equation — must know!
v = f × λ
v = wave speed (m/s)  ·  f = frequency (Hz)  ·  λ = wavelength (m)
Speed of EM waves in air = 300 000 km/s = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
📐 Worked Example

Calculate the wavelength of microwaves of frequency 2400 MHz.
f = 2400 MHz = 2 400 000 000 Hz = 2.4 × 10⁹ Hz
λ = v ÷ f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2.4 × 10⁹ = 0.125 m

Wave Types in Detail

🌈 White Light & Visible Spectrum

  • White light = all colours of the visible spectrum mixed together.
  • Wavelength increases from violet → red.
  • A prism splits white light into its spectrum (dispersion).
  • You see the spectrum in a rainbow.
  • Shades and colours of light affect photographs.

Cameras:

  • Film camera: light focused onto light-sensitive film → developed to make image.
  • Digital camera / phone: light focused onto a sensor with millions of pixels (light-sensitive cells) → stored electronically.

🌡️ Infrared Radiation

  • All objects emit infrared radiation.
  • The hotter the object, the more infrared it emits.
  • Absorbed by skin — can damage, burn, or kill skin cells.

Devices that use infrared:

Optical Fibres

Use IR instead of visible light. IR is absorbed less by glass fibres → signals travel further.

Remote Controls

Transmit signals as infrared pulses when you press a button.

IR Scanners (Medicine)

Detect hot spots on the body — hot areas can mean unhealthy tissue.

Night-Vision Cameras

Detect IR emitted by people/animals in the dark.

IR heats quickly: electric heaters, halogen hobs (designed to emit much more IR than ordinary hobs).

📡 Microwaves

  • Shorter wavelength than radio waves.
  • Can pass through the atmosphere → reach satellites.
  • Used for: satellite TV, mobile phone signals, communications.
Microwave oven: Microwaves penetrate food and are absorbed by water molecules, heating the food. The oven itself does NOT absorb microwaves (no water molecules in it) — it doesn't heat up.

📻 Radio Waves

  • Frequencies: 300 000 Hz → 3000 million Hz.
  • Used for: radio, TV, mobile phone signals.
  • Can replace cables to connect computers to printers/mice.
  • Bluetooth: communicates up to ~10 metres without cables.

Microwaves vs Radio Waves

📡 Microwaves

  • Shorter wavelength
  • Higher frequency
  • Pass through atmosphere
  • Satellite TV & phones
  • Ovens (water absorption)

📻 Radio Waves

  • Longer wavelength
  • Lower frequency
  • Radio & TV broadcasting
  • Bluetooth / wireless
  • Mobile phones (near-microwave)

Hazards

⚠️ Infrared Hazard

Can cause skin burns. Damages, burns, or kills skin cells.

⚠️ Microwave & Radio Hazard

Penetrate the body and heat internal parts of the body.

⚠️ Key Distinction

Infrared → surface burns. Microwaves & radio waves → internal heating (they penetrate the body). Different types of EM radiation are hazardous in different ways.

Exam Tips

✏️ Exam Tip 1

When asked about microwave ovens, explain that water molecules in the food absorb the microwaves — not the oven itself. This is why the food heats up but the oven stays cool.

✏️ Exam Tip 2

Remember: microwaves and radio waves are hazardous because they penetrate the body and heat it internally. Infrared is hazardous because it causes surface skin burns. Don't mix these up!

✏️ Exam Tip 3

For wavelength calculations, always convert MHz to Hz first (× 10⁶), then use λ = v ÷ f with v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. Show every step for full marks.
