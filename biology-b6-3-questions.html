B6.3 Discovering drugs

Questions

📖 Revision ✏️ Questions
1
Describe how Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin.
[3 marks]
2
Suggest reasons for using synthetic forms of drugs rather than using plant extracts directly.
[4 marks]
3
Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of looking for new antibiotic compounds in living organisms, based on the example of the noni fruit.
[6 marks]
4
Aspirin was originally derived from willow bark. Explain why a synthetic version was later developed instead of continuing to use willow bark extract.
[2 marks]
5
Describe the roles of Chain and Florey in the development of penicillin as a medicine.
[3 marks]
6
In 2015, scientists announced a completely new antibiotic from soil bacteria that destroyed all bacteria tested — including MRSA. However, scientists were cautious about announcing a breakthrough. Explain why.
[3 marks]
7
Give two examples of drugs that originated from plants. For each, state what it is used to treat.
[4 marks]
8
Explain why ancient Egyptians using mouldy bread on septic wounds was actually effective, even though they had no scientific understanding of why.
[2 marks]
9
Explain why only ~1% of soil microorganisms can be studied in the lab, and how scientists have overcome this problem.
[2 marks]
10
Explain why Fleming's discovery of penicillin was partly the result of good luck, and partly the result of good scientific thinking.
[3 marks]
