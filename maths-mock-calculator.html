Question 3[4 marks]

Solve 3x² + 2x − 8 = 0 using the quadratic formula. Give your answers to 2 decimal places.

Mark Scheme — Q3 [4 marks]

  • [1] a=3, b=2, c=−8: discriminant = 2² − 4(3)(−8) = 4 + 96 = 100
  • [1] x = (−2 ± √100) / 6 = (−2 ± 10)/6
  • [1] x = 8/6 = 1.33 (2dp)
  • [1] or x = −12/6 = −2.00

Question 5[3 marks]

Given that v² = u² + 2as, find v when u = 12, a = 3 and s = 20. Give your answer to 3 significant figures (v > 0).

Mark Scheme — Q5 [3 marks]

  • [1] v² = 12² + 2(3)(20) = 144 + 120
  • [1] v² = 264, v = √264
  • [1] = 16.2 (3 s.f.)

Question 6[4 marks]

Find the equation of the straight line passing through (1.5, 4) and (3.5, 10). Give your answer in the form y = mx + c.

Mark Scheme — Q6 [4 marks]

  • [1] Gradient = (10−4)/(3.5−1.5) = 6/2 = 3
  • [1] Substitutes a point: 4 = 3(1.5) + c
  • [1] c = 4 − 4.5 = −0.5
  • [1] y = 3x − 0.5
Ratio, Proportion & Rates of Change
Question 7[4 marks]

Share £742 between A, B and C in the ratio 3 : 4 : 7. State the amount each person receives.

Mark Scheme — Q7 [4 marks]

  • [1] Total parts = 3+4+7 = 14
  • [1] Value of one part = 742 ÷ 14 = £53
  • [1] Multiplies by each ratio part: 3×53, 4×53, 7×53
  • [1] A = £159, B = £212, C = £371
Ratio, Proportion & Rates of Change Grade 7+
Question 8[4 marks]

y is directly proportional to √x. When x = 16, y = 20. Find y when x = 25.

Mark Scheme — Q8 [4 marks]

  • [1] y = k√x, substitutes: 20 = k√16 = 4k
  • [1] k = 5, so y = 5√x
  • [1] When x=25: y = 5√25 = 5×5
  • [1] = 25

Statistics & Probability Grade 7+
Question 15[4 marks]

The table shows the scores of 30 students in a test:

Score, x012345
Frequency, f259743
Calculate the mean score.

Mark Scheme — Q15 [4 marks]

  • [1] Σf = 2+5+9+7+4+3 = 30
  • [1] Σfx = 0+5+18+21+16+15 = 75
  • [1] Mean = Σfx ÷ Σf = 75 ÷ 30
  • [1] = 2.5
