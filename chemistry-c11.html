C11.2

Our evolving atmosphere

Carbon dioxide fell as carbon became locked in living material, oceans, carbonate rocks and fossil fuels. Nitrogen accumulated because it is relatively unreactive.

The atmosphere today

N₂ ≈ 78%
O₂ ≈ 21%
other

Argon is about 0.9%; carbon dioxide is a very small proportion (the textbook gives about 0.04%), plus variable water vapour and traces of other gases.

Photosynthesis

Removes CO₂ and produces O₂.

Rocks & fuels

Carbon locked into carbonate rocks, coal, crude oil and natural gas.

Oceans

CO₂ dissolves; carbonates form and become sediments.

C11.4

Global climate change

AQA expects potential effects, discussion of scale/risk/environmental implications, carbon footprints, reduction strategies and their limitations.

Sea level

Melting land ice + thermal expansion → flooding and coastal erosion.

Weather & water

Changes in rainfall and more/extreme weather events.

Ecosystems & food

Species distributions, habitats and food production can change.

Carbon footprint

total CO₂ + other greenhouse gases emitted over the full life cycle of a product, service or event

Try it · choose the best lever

Which action directly reduces fossil-fuel CO₂ emissions?

Why solutions are not simple

Renewables, efficiency, carbon capture and storage, reforestation and lower-carbon transport can reduce emissions, but cost, infrastructure, technology, international agreements, economic interests and lifestyle choices can limit action.
