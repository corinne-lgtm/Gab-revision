AQA GCSE Biology · B9 Respiration

B9.3 Anaerobic respiration

Questions

📖 Revision ✏️ Questions
1
If you exercise very hard or for a long time, your muscles begin to ache and do not work effectively. Explain why.
[4 marks]
2
a) Define anaerobic respiration. [1] b) Explain how anaerobic respiration differs between animals, plants, and yeast. In each case explain the benefits to the organism of being able to respire in this way. [6] c) Write the word equation for anaerobic respiration in animals, plants, and yeast. [3]
[10 marks]
3
(Higher) If you exercise vigorously, you often puff and pant for some time after you stop. Explain what is happening.
[4 marks]
4
Explain why less energy is transferred during anaerobic respiration than during aerobic respiration.
[2 marks]
5
Describe one commercial use of fermentation by yeast and explain the role of anaerobic respiration in this process.
[3 marks]
6
A 100 m sprinter uses a lot of anaerobic respiration during the race. Explain why the sprinter's oxygen debt may be large despite the race lasting only a few seconds.
[3 marks]
7
Explain why a fit person's breathing rate returns to normal more quickly after exercise than an unfit person's breathing rate.
[3 marks]
8
Plants sometimes carry out anaerobic respiration when they are growing in waterlogged soil. Suggest why this might eventually cause them to die if the soil remains waterlogged.
[3 marks]
9
Lactic acid produced during anaerobic respiration must eventually be broken down. Describe what happens to lactic acid after exercise, including the organ involved.
[3 marks]
10
Compare aerobic and anaerobic respiration in animal cells. Include in your answer: reactants, products, where it takes place, and relative energy transferred.
[5 marks]
