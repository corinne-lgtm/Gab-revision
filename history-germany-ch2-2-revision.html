2.2 What was the Weimar Republic?

How Germany changed from Kaiser rule to a democratic republic — and why the new system had problems from the start.

🏠 Main hub 📚 History index 📚 Germany index ✏️ Quiz rapide
Germany became a democracy in 1919 —
but the new republic was born in chaos, with enemies on the left and right.
🗳️

Democratic Republic

Germany would now choose leaders by voting, instead of being ruled by a Kaiser.

🏛️

Weimar Constitution

A new system with a President, Chancellor, Reichstag and voting rights.

⚠️

Weak start

Many people blamed the new leaders for defeat, the armistice and disorder.

🔥

Political violence

Communists and right-wing groups both challenged the new republic.

Government structure: who did what?

PeopleVoted for the President and Reichstag.
PresidentHead of state. Could use emergency powers under Article 48.
ChancellorRan the government day-to-day. Needed Reichstag support.
ReichstagParliament. Discussed and introduced laws.

Strengths vs weaknesses

✅

Strengths

  • More democratic than Kaiser rule.
  • Men and women over 20 could vote.
  • Basic freedoms were promised, such as free speech.
  • Proportional representation gave smaller parties seats.
❌

Weaknesses

  • Proportional representation made coalitions common and unstable.
  • Article 48 could let the President rule in an emergency.
  • Many Germans disliked democracy or blamed the republic for defeat.
  • Extreme groups tried to overthrow the new government.

Political enemies

🌹

Spartacists

Left-wing communist group. Wanted workers’ councils and a communist Germany. Led by Rosa Luxemburg and Karl Liebknecht.

🪖

Free Corps

Right-wing ex-soldiers. They hated communists and used violence to crush the Spartacist Revolt.

⚖️

Centre ground

Ebert and the SPD wanted a democratic republic, but were attacked by both extremes.
