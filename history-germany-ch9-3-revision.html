AQA GCSE History · Germany 1890–1945

9.3 How were the arts and culture of Germany affected by Nazi rule?

🏠 Main hub 📚 History index 📚 Germany index ✏️ Quiz rapide

Big idea

🎭

Culture became political

The Nazis did not see art, music, films, theatre, literature or newspapers as neutral entertainment. They used culture to spread Nazi beliefs and remove ideas they disliked.

📢

Goebbels controlled culture

Joseph Goebbels set up the Chamber of Culture. Artists, writers, musicians, actors and filmmakers had to belong to it if they wanted to work legally.

🚫

Censorship was central

Work that was Jewish, modern, critical of the Nazis, foreign-influenced or politically unacceptable was banned, destroyed or controlled.

The Chamber of Culture

The Chamber of Culture was run by Goebbels. Its job was to make sure that German culture supported Nazi ideas.

What it controlled

  • Art and exhibitions
  • Cinema and film-making
  • Music and concerts
  • Theatre and plays
  • Literature, books and newspapers

Who was excluded?

  • Jewish artists and writers
  • Political opponents
  • People who refused to support Nazi ideas
  • Artists linked to modern or experimental culture

Why this mattered

If people were not allowed to join the Chamber of Culture, they often could not work. This gave the Nazis control over careers as well as ideas.

Cinema and film

How Nazis used cinema

  • Films had to be approved before release.
  • News reports before films showed Nazi achievements.
  • Films often praised Hitler, soldiers, patriotism and loyalty.
  • Some films spread antisemitic messages.

Example: Jud Süß

Jud Süß was a 1940 Nazi propaganda film. It presented Jewish people in a hateful and false way, showing how cinema could be used to spread racism and antisemitism.

Music, theatre and literature

🎼

Music

  • Music was expected to be German and traditional.
  • Classical German composers such as Bach, Beethoven, Mozart and Wagner were approved.
  • Jazz was disliked and attacked because Nazis associated it with Black American culture.
  • Jewish composers such as Mendelssohn and Mahler were banned.
🎟️

Theatre

  • Modern plays and plays critical of Nazi ideas were banned.
  • Plays by approved German writers such as Goethe and Schiller were encouraged.
  • Cabaret clubs were shut down because they often mocked politics and authority.
📚

Literature

  • Goebbels created lists of banned books.
  • Books by Jewish, socialist, pacifist or foreign writers were removed.
  • In 1933, students and Nazis burned around 20,000 books in Berlin.
  • Mein Kampf became a best-selling book in Nazi Germany.

Why did the Nazis control culture?

To spread Nazi beliefs

Culture repeated Nazi messages about loyalty, obedience, nationalism, antisemitism and the greatness of Hitler.

To remove opposition

Books, music, films and plays that challenged Nazi ideas were banned, so people saw fewer alternative viewpoints.

To shape everyday life

Because entertainment was part of daily life, propaganda could reach people even when they were relaxing at the cinema, theatre or concert hall.

Key points to remember

Goebbels Controlled propaganda and culture.
Chamber of Culture Decided who could work in cultural jobs.
Censorship Removed unacceptable ideas.
Book burning Destroyed banned books publicly.
