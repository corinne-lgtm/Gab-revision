Environmental Health Officer (EHO)

A focused, interactive practice area for the higher-mark questions highlighted by your teacher. Original practice questions, not an official past paper.

What you must be able to explain

An Environmental Health Officer helps protect public health by checking food businesses comply with food safety requirements. For a developed answer, say what the EHO does, why and what that means for customers or the business.

Inspections

Check cleanliness, food handling, pest control and temperature controls.

Investigations

Investigate complaints and suspected outbreaks; gather evidence or samples.

Advice

Tell businesses what needs to improve and how to reduce risks.

Enforcement

Use proportionate legal powers where necessary; serious risks may require urgent action.

Remember: EHOs can inspect and enforce food safety law. They do not personally award Michelin stars. Food Hygiene Rating Scheme ratings follow inspections in participating areas.

Build a higher-mark answer

Use the structure Point → Explain → Impact. Avoid listing responsibilities without explaining their purpose.

Point

An EHO inspects food storage temperatures.

Explain + Impact

This identifies unsafe storage that could allow bacteria to multiply, helping prevent food poisoning.

Useful exam command words

State: give a fact. Describe: say what happens. Explain: give reasons or consequences. Discuss: consider relevant aspects in a developed response.

A final mark depends on the exact question and marking criteria; no fixed writing formula guarantees full marks.

Check your knowledge

Choose one answer for each question, then read the explanation.

Exam practice

Feedback is a self-check based on key ideas, not an official or reliable predicted exam mark. Compare your answer with the model response and teacher's mark scheme.
