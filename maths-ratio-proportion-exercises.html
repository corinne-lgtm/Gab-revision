Ratios
Question 1[4 marks]

A sum of money is shared between A, B and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. B receives £18 more than A. Work out the total amount of money shared.

Mark Scheme — Q1 [4 marks]

  • [1] Sets parts as 2k, 3k, 5k
  • [1] B − A = k = 18, so k = 18
  • [1] Total = 10k
  • [1] Total = 10 × 18 = £180
Equations of Proportionality Grade 7+
Question 2[5 marks]

y is directly proportional to x². When x = 3, y = 45.

(a) [2 marks]
Find an equation connecting y and x.
(b) [1 mark]
Find y when x = 6.
(c) [2 marks]
Find the positive value of x when y = 320.

Mark Scheme — Q2 [5 marks]

  • [1] (a) y = kx², substitutes 45 = k(3²) = 9k
  • [1] (a) k = 5, so y = 5x²
  • [1] (b) y = 5 × 6² = 180
  • [1] (c) 320 = 5x² → x² = 64
  • [1] (c) x = 8
Percentages — Change & Reverse
Question 3[5 marks]
(a) [3 marks]
A laptop's price was reduced by 15% in a sale to £510. Work out the original price of the laptop.
(b) [2 marks]
A different item costs £80 and its price increases by 12%. Work out the new price.

Mark Scheme — Q3 [5 marks]

  • [1] (a) Recognises £510 represents 85% of the original price
  • [1] (a) Original = 510 ÷ 0.85
  • [1] (a) = £600
  • [1] (b) New price = 80 × 1.12
  • [1] (b) = £89.60
