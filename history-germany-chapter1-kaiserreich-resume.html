Germany 1890–1914: Kaiser Wilhelm II

Visual Revision Summary

Big Picture

  • Germany became unified in 1871 under Prussian leadership.
  • Kaiser Wilhelm II ruled from 1888.
  • Germany combined industrial growth with strong militarism.
  • Political power remained heavily controlled by the Kaiser.
  • Socialism and the SPD grew as workers demanded reform.
  • Weltpolitik aimed to build Germany into a global empire.

Key Timeline

1871
German Empire created
1888
Kaiser Wilhelm II becomes ruler
1898–1912
Naval Laws expand navy
1914
Germany enters First World War

How Germany Was Ruled

  • Kaiser = supreme authority
  • Chancellor = chief minister
  • Bundesrat = federal council
  • Reichstag = elected parliament, but limited power
  • Army and foreign policy mainly controlled by Kaiser

Key Problems

  • Rapid industrialisation created wealth but also poor working conditions
  • Growing support for socialism worried elites
  • Military spending increased taxes and debt
  • Naval rivalry with Britain increased tension

Exam Focus

  • Strengths and weaknesses of Kaiser’s rule
  • Reasons for industrial success
  • Impact of militarism
  • Rise of socialism
  • Weltpolitik and naval expansion
