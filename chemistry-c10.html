C10.1

Pure substances and mixtures

In chemistry, pure means one element or one compound, not mixed with another substance.

Pure

Characteristic fixed melting and boiling points.

Impure

Usually changes state over a range; impurities often lower melting point and raise boiling point.

Formulation

A useful mixture designed for a purpose, with components in measured proportions.

ObservationEvidence
Sharp fixed point matching reference datasupports purity and identity
Melting over a rangesupports impurity / mixture

C10.2 · Required Practical 12

Analysing chromatograms

Separation depends on distribution between stationary paper and moving solvent.

Stationary phase

Paper. Stronger attraction → less travel.

Mobile phase

Solvent. Stronger attraction/solubility → more travel.

Interpretation

Mixtures can give several spots. Pure compounds give a single spot.

pencil baseline

Rf = distance moved by substance ÷ distance moved by solvent

Measure to the centre of the spot from the baseline.
