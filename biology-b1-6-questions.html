AQA GCSE Biology · B1 Cell structure and transport

B1.6 Diffusion

Questions

📖 Revision ✏️ Questions
1
Define the process of diffusion in terms of the particles involved.
[2 marks]
2
Explain why diffusion takes place faster when there is an increase in temperature.
[3 marks]
3
Explain why so many cells have folded membranes along at least one surface.
[2 marks]
4
What is a concentration gradient? How does the size of the concentration gradient affect the rate of diffusion?
[3 marks]
5
Describe the process of diffusion occurring when digested food products move from your gut into the bloodstream.
[3 marks]
6
Describe the process of diffusion when carbon dioxide moves from the blood in the capillaries of your lungs to the air in the lungs. Include any adaptations that are involved.
[3 marks]
7
Male moths can track down a mate from up to 3 miles away because of chemicals produced by the female. Describe how this is an example of diffusion.
[3 marks]
8
What is meant by 'net movement'? Why do particles still move from low to high concentration even during diffusion?
[2 marks]
9
Name three factors that affect the rate of diffusion and for each one explain how increasing it affects the rate.
[6 marks]
10
Oxygen passes from the air in the lungs into red blood cells by diffusion. Explain how the body is adapted to make this process as efficient as possible.
[4 marks]
