Indices and Simplifying
Question 1[4 marks]
(a) [2 marks]
Simplify fully: (2x³y²)³
(b) [2 marks]
Simplify fully: (24x⁵y⁷) ÷ (6x²y⁹)

Mark Scheme — Q1 [4 marks]

  • [1] (a) Cubes each factor: 2³=8, (x³)³=x⁹, (y²)³=y⁶
  • [1] (a) = 8x⁹y⁶
  • [1] (b) Divides coefficients and subtracts indices: 24÷6=4, x⁵⁻²=x³, y⁷⁻⁹=y⁻²
  • [1] (b) = 4x³/y² (equivalently 4x³y⁻²)
Negative and Fractional Indices Grade 7+
Question 2[4 marks]
(a) [2 marks]
Evaluate 8^(2/3)
(b) [2 marks]
Evaluate 49^(−1/2)

Mark Scheme — Q2 [4 marks]

  • [1] (a) Takes cube root of 8: ∛8 = 2
  • [1] (a) Squares the result: 2² = 4
  • [1] (b) Takes square root of 49 and inverts: 49^(1/2) = 7, so 49^(−1/2) = 1/7
  • [1] (b) Final answer: 1/7
Solving Quadratics — Completing the Square & Formula Grade 7+
Question 3[6 marks]
(a) [3 marks]
Solve x² + 6x + 2 = 0 by completing the square. Give your answers in simplified surd form.
(b) [3 marks]
Solve 2x² − 5x − 3 = 0 using the quadratic formula. Give your answers to 2 decimal places.

Mark Scheme — Q3 [6 marks]

  • [1] (a) (x+3)² − 9 + 2 = 0
  • [1] (a) (x+3)² = 7
  • [1] (a) x = −3 ± √7
  • [1] (b) a=2, b=−5, c=−3 substituted: x = [5 ± √(25+24)] / 4
  • [1] (b) √49 = 7, so x = (5+7)/4 or x = (5−7)/4
  • [1] (b) x = 3.00 or x = −0.50
Rearranging Formulas
Question 4[3 marks]

Make x the subject of:   y = (3x + 2) / (x − 5)

Mark Scheme — Q4 [3 marks]

  • [1] Multiplies both sides by (x − 5): y(x−5) = 3x + 2 → yx − 5y = 3x + 2
  • [1] Collects x terms on one side: yx − 3x = 2 + 5y, then factorises: x(y−3) = 2 + 5y
  • [1] x = (2 + 5y) / (y − 3)
Simultaneous Equations Grade 7+
Question 5[5 marks]

Solve the simultaneous equations:   y = x + 1  and  x² + y² = 13

Substitute the linear equation into the quadratic one, then solve.

Mark Scheme — Q5 [5 marks]

  • [1] Substitutes y = x+1 into x² + y² = 13: x² + (x+1)² = 13
  • [1] Expands and rearranges to: 2x² + 2x − 12 = 0, i.e. x² + x − 6 = 0
  • [1] Factorises: (x+3)(x−2) = 0 → x = −3 or x = 2
  • [1] Finds corresponding y values using y = x+1
  • [1] Correct pairs: x = −3, y = −2 and x = 2, y = 3

Forming and Solving Equations
Question 7[4 marks]

A rectangle has length (3x + 1) cm and width (x + 2) cm. The perimeter of the rectangle is 38 cm.

Form an equation, solve it for x, and hence find the area of the rectangle.

Mark Scheme — Q7 [4 marks]

  • [1] Forms the correct equation: 2[(3x+1)+(x+2)] = 38
  • [1] Simplifies: 8x + 6 = 38
  • [1] Solves: x = 4
  • [1] Length = 13 cm, width = 6 cm, Area = 78 cm²
Error Intervals
Question 8[3 marks]

A length, l, is recorded as 6.4 cm to 1 decimal place. Write down the error interval for l. Then, a mass, m, is recorded as 250 g to 2 significant figures — write down the error interval for m.

Mark Scheme — Q8 [3 marks]

  • [1] Half the unit of accuracy is 0.05, giving 6.35 ≤ l < 6.45
  • [1] Recognises 2 s.f. on 250 means the unit of accuracy is 10
  • [1] 245 ≤ m < 255
