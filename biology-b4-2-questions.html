AQA GCSE Biology · B4 Organising animals and plants

B4.2 The blood vessels

Questions

📖 Revision ✏️ Questions
1
State the function of each of the following blood vessels. Describe how the structure of each blood vessel relates to its function: a) arteries b) veins c) capillaries.
[8 marks]
2
a) Describe how the heart, arteries, veins, and capillaries are linked together in the circulatory system. b) Describe what happens in the capillaries in a cell.
[4 marks]
3
Fish have a single circulation system. The blood goes from the heart, through the gills, around the body, and back to the heart. Describe the disadvantages of a single circulation system like this for an active land mammal such as a human being.
[4 marks]
4
Explain why veins have valves but arteries do not.
[3 marks]
5
Arteries have thick, muscular, elastic walls. Explain why each of these features is important for their function.
[3 marks]
6
Describe the two separate circulatory systems in the human double circulatory system. State the advantage of this system over a single one.
[3 marks]
7
A student says: "The pulmonary artery carries oxygenated blood." Is this correct? Explain your answer.
[2 marks]
8
Explain how blood is moved through veins back towards the heart, given that blood pressure in veins is low.
[2 marks]
9
Explain why capillaries are so important for supplying the needs of individual cells.
[3 marks]
10
If an artery is cut, blood spurts out rapidly and is dangerous. If a vein is cut, blood flows out more steadily. Explain why.
[2 marks]
