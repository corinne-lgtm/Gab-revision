B6.1 Vaccination

How your immune system works and how vaccination protects you against disease.

Vaccination trains your immune system without making you ill — so when the real pathogen arrives, your body responds immediately.

How the immune system works

🔵

Antigens

Every cell has unique proteins on its surface called antigens. The antigens on pathogens are different to those on your own cells — this is how your immune system recognises them as foreign.

💉

Antibodies

White blood cells produce specific antibodies that join up with and destroy a particular antigen. You need a unique antibody for each type of pathogen.

🧠

Memory cells

Some white blood cells (memory cells) remember the right antibody for a pathogen. If that pathogen returns, memory cells can produce the correct antibody very quickly — before you feel ill.

⚠️ Study tip: High antibody levels don't stay forever. Immunity is the ability of memory cells to produce the right antibodies quickly — not a constantly high level of antibodies.

How vaccination works — 3 steps

1
Small amount of dead/inactive pathogen introduced into body (often by injection)
2
White blood cells produce antibodies to destroy the antigens — without you getting ill
3
Memory cells remain — if the real pathogen enters, antibodies are made rapidly → you are immune
💡 Vaccines work against both bacterial and viral diseases — because both types of pathogen have antigens that the immune system can recognise (e.g. tetanus and diphtheria = bacterial; MMR, polio = viral). Smallpox has been completely wiped out by vaccination.

Herd immunity

👥

If a large proportion of the population is immune to a disease, the spread of the pathogen is greatly reduced — even non-immune people are protected because there are so few people to catch it from. This is herd immunity.

If vaccination rates fall too low, herd immunity is lost and the disease can return. This happened in the UK in the 1970s with whooping cough — when a vaccine scare cut vaccination rates from over 80% to ~30%, thousands of children got whooping cough and some died. The WHO wants 95% of children vaccinated for measles herd immunity.
