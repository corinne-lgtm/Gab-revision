👨‍🍳 The operation of the kitchen

Back of house dress code ("chef's whites")

Double-breasted jacket, long trousers, head covering, apron, non-slip toe-protected shoes. Protects the wearer from injury and protects food from hair/sweat.

Kitchen workflow areas

AreaPurpose
DeliveryAt the entrance — deliveries checked against the order, temperature of high-risk food recorded
StorageCool area (fridges/freezers, high-risk food, fruit/veg) + dry area (cans, dry goods)
StaffingStaff change into work clothes; toilets & hand-washing; sometimes a break lounge
PreparationSeparate areas for meat/poultry, fish, fruit/veg, pastries/desserts
CookingHot wet area (soups, sauces, steaming) + dry area (roasting, baking, grilling, frying)
ServingPlating hot/cold food; waiters collect orders from "the pass"
CleaningSeparate area; dirty crockery/cutlery/pans cleaned & stored
WasteSeparate area outside; food/recyclable/non-recyclable waste sorted into correct bins

🔧 Kitchen equipment

CategoryExamples
Large equipmentWalk-in fridge/freezer, blast chiller, floor-standing mixer, conventional oven, deep fat fryer, hot water urn, bain-marie, grill/salamander, pass-through dishwasher
Mechanical/specialistWeighing scales, electric whisk, food processor, blender, mincer, meat slicer, temperature probe, conveyor toaster, panini maker, pizza oven, sous vide, pasta maker
Small equipmentMixing bowls, jugs/spoons, whisks, sieves, knives, chopping boards, graters, pots/pans, baking trays, plates/glassware
Cleaning & safetyDetergents, scouring pads, mops, waste bags/bins, fire extinguisher/blanket, smoke/CO₂ alarm, first aid box, oven gloves

Good quality equipment costs more upfront but lasts longer and electrical items cost less to run — saving money long-term.

🚪 Front of house operation

Restaurant workflow

Reception → seating/dining area (divided into stations) → counter service area → bar → equipment station (cutlery, condiments) → toilets → safety equipment (first aid, fire extinguisher easily accessed).

Hotel workflow

Reception (check-in, keys) → lobby/waiting area → stairs/lifts → toilets.

Administration

Admin staff track: staff employment/training records, stock orders/deliveries/invoices, health & safety documents, financial info, customer feedback, advertising.

Front of house dress code

Clean & ironed clothing/uniform, minimal jewellery/perfume/make-up, good personal hygiene, name badges.

🙋 Customer requirements

Customers fall into three groups: business, leisure, local residents.

Customer typeTypical needs
BusinessMeeting catering, early breakfast, 24hr room service, conference facilities (Wi-Fi, projector), quiet work floor, express check-in/out, loyalty points
LeisureMini bar, room service, restaurant/bar, dietary options, children's menu, en-suite, free Wi-Fi, concierge, cots/extra bedding
Local residentsUse facilities (restaurant, bar, spa, golf) without overnight stay; provider must manage noise & parking

Customer rights & inclusion

By law, no business can discriminate based on: age, disability, sexual orientation, ethnicity, gender, race/culture, pregnancy & maternity.

🎯 Meeting specific requirements

What affects customer needs

  • Lifestyle: budget, eating patterns, hobbies/interests
  • Nutritional needs: menus showing nutritional info to help choices
  • Dietary needs: coeliac, vegetarian, vegan, children's options
  • Time available: fast food vs leisurely meal

Customer demographics (used for marketing & USP)

Age, location, accessibility (parking/mobility), money available, level of local competition.

Customer expectations

Polite efficient service, value for money, keeping up with trends (apps), awareness of competitors, matching media reviews, eco-friendliness, seasonal/local ingredients.
