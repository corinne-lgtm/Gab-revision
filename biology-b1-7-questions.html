AQA GCSE Biology · B1 Cell structure and transport

B1.7 Osmosis

Questions

📖 Revision ✏️ Questions
1
Explain the difference between osmosis and diffusion.
[2 marks]
2
Define the following terms: i) isotonic solution ii) hypotonic solution iii) hypertonic solution.
[3 marks]
3
What is a partially permeable membrane? Give an example of one in a living organism.
[2 marks]
4
A dilute solution of sugar has a high concentration of water. Explain in which direction water will move by osmosis if a cell is placed in a dilute sugar solution.
[2 marks]
5
What happens to an animal cell placed in a very concentrated (hypertonic) solution? Explain why.
[3 marks]
6
What happens to an animal cell placed in a very dilute (hypotonic) solution? Explain why.
[3 marks]
7
Explain how osmosis helps to maintain the cytoplasm of plant and body cells at a specific concentration.
[2 marks]
8
Explain why it is so important for the cells of the human body that the solute concentration of the fluid surrounding the cells is kept as constant as possible.
[4 marks]
9
Animals that live in fresh water have a constant problem with their water balance. The single-celled organism Amoeba has a special vacuole in its cell. The vacuole fills with water and then moves to the outside of the cell and bursts, forming a new vacuole straight away. Explain in terms of osmosis why the Amoeba needs one of these vacuoles.
[4 marks]
10
A student places a piece of potato into a very salty solution. Predict and explain what will happen to the mass of the potato.
[4 marks]
