Round 2 · Exam answer technique

Important: Elizabethan England is a British depth study; Conflict and Tension is a wider-world period study. Their actual AQA question formats are not identical. The two prompts below are practice for explanation and chronology, not a replica of the entire paper.

Elizabeth · Explain two consequences (practice)

Explain two ways in which religious divisions threatened Elizabeth's authority.

Possible evidence: the Northern Rebellion, papal excommunication, missionary priests, plots involving Mary, Queen of Scots, and changing relations with Spain. Use precise dates only when confident. Explain the consequence of each example.

Conflict · 8-mark style account (practice)

Write an account of how events from the remilitarisation of the Rhineland in 1936 to the invasion of Poland in 1939 increased international tension.

Include specific years and decisions; link each step to Hitler's increased confidence and the failure of deterrence. Finish at Poland and explain why Britain and France declared war.
