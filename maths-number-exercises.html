Question 3[4 marks]

A car travels 180 km in 2 hours 15 minutes.

(a) Average speed [2 marks]
Work out the average speed of the car, in km/h.
(b) Fuel used [2 marks]
The car uses fuel at a rate of 6.25 km per litre. Work out how many litres are used for the 180 km journey.

Mark Scheme — Q3 [4 marks]

  • [1] 2 hours 15 minutes = 2.25 hours
  • [1] Speed = 180 ÷ 2.25 = 80 km/h
  • [1] Litres = 180 ÷ 6.25
  • [1] = 28.8 litres
