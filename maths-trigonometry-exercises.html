Pythagoras' Theorem
Question 1[4 marks]
(a) [2 marks]
A ladder of length 6.5 m leans against a vertical wall. The foot of the ladder is 2.5 m from the base of the wall. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?
(b) [2 marks]
A ship sails 8 km due north, then 15 km due east. Find the direct distance from its starting point.

Mark Scheme — Q1 [4 marks]

  • [1] (a) height² = 6.5² − 2.5² = 42.25 − 6.25 = 36
  • [1] (a) height = 6 m
  • [1] (b) distance² = 8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289
  • [1] (b) distance = 17 km
Trigonometry for a Side
Question 2[4 marks]

In a right-angled triangle, angle A = 42° and the hypotenuse is 15 cm. Give your answers to 3 significant figures.

(a) [2 marks]
Find the length of the side opposite angle A.
(b) [2 marks]
Find the length of the side adjacent to angle A.

Mark Scheme — Q2 [4 marks]

  • [1] (a) opposite = 15 × sin(42°)
  • [1] (a) = 10.0 cm (3 s.f.)
  • [1] (b) adjacent = 15 × cos(42°)
  • [1] (b) = 11.1 cm (3 s.f.)
Trigonometry for an Angle
Question 3[4 marks]
(a) [2 marks]
A right-angled triangle has opposite side 7 cm and adjacent side 9 cm. Find angle θ between the adjacent side and the hypotenuse, to 1 decimal place.
(b) [2 marks]
A different right-angled triangle has hypotenuse 12 cm and opposite side 5 cm. Find the angle θ, to 1 decimal place.

Mark Scheme — Q3 [4 marks]

  • [1] (a) tanθ = 7/9 = 0.7778
  • [1] (a) θ = tan⁻¹(0.7778) = 37.9°
  • [1] (b) sinθ = 5/12 = 0.4167
  • [1] (b) θ = sin⁻¹(0.4167) = 24.6°
Trigonometry in 3D Grade 7+
Question 4[6 marks]

A cuboid ABCDEFGH has length 8 cm, width 6 cm and height 5 cm, where ABCD is the base and G is directly above C.

(a) [2 marks]
Find the length of the diagonal AC of the base.
(b) [2 marks]
Find the length of the space diagonal AG, to 3 significant figures.
(c) [2 marks]
Find the angle that AG makes with the base ABCD, to 1 decimal place.

Mark Scheme — Q4 [6 marks]

  • [1] (a) AC² = 8² + 6² = 100
  • [1] (a) AC = 10 cm
  • [1] (b) AG² = AC² + 5² = 100 + 25 = 125
  • [1] (b) AG = √125 = 11.2 cm (3 s.f.)
  • [1] (c) tan(angle) = height/AC = 5/10 = 0.5
  • [1] (c) angle = tan⁻¹(0.5) = 26.6°
Mixed Exam-Style Problem
Question 5[6 marks]

A vertical flagpole stands on horizontal ground. From a point P on the ground, 20 m from the base of the pole, the angle of elevation to the top of the pole is 35°.

(a) [2 marks]
Work out the height of the flagpole, to 3 significant figures.
(b) [2 marks]
A wire runs from the top of the pole to point P. Find the length of the wire, to 3 significant figures.
(c) [2 marks]
Mary walks from P directly away from the pole until the angle of elevation is 20°. How far has she walked, to 3 significant figures?

Mark Scheme — Q5 [6 marks]

  • [1] (a) height = 20 × tan(35°)
  • [1] (a) = 14.0 m (3 s.f.)
  • [1] (b) wire = 20 ÷ cos(35°), or Pythagoras with 20 and 14.0
  • [1] (b) = 24.4 m (3 s.f.)
  • [1] (c) New distance from pole = 14.0 ÷ tan(20°) = 38.46 m
  • [1] (c) Distance walked = 38.46 − 20 = 18.5 m (3 s.f.)
