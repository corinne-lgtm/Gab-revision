Stem and Leaf
Question 1[5 marks]

The stem-and-leaf diagram shows the time (in minutes) taken by 16 students to complete a puzzle.

StemLeaf
12  5  8  9
20  1  3  6  7  9
32  4  5  8
41  3

Key: 1 | 2 means 12 minutes

(a) [2 marks]
Find the number of students who took less than 30 minutes.
(b) [3 marks]
Find the median time taken.

Mark Scheme — Q1 [5 marks]

  • [1] (a) Counts students in stems 1 and 2: 4 + 6
  • [1] (a) = 10 students
  • [1] (b) Recognises n = 16, so median is the mean of the 8th and 9th values
  • [1] (b) Identifies the 8th value as 26 and 9th value as 27
  • [1] (b) Median = (26+27)/2 = 26.5 minutes
Pie Charts and Bar Charts (Composite & Dual)
Question 2[6 marks]
(a) [2 marks]
A pie chart represents the favourite sports of 180 students. The "Football" sector has an angle of 100°. Work out how many students chose football.
(b) [4 marks]
A survey of 60 students recorded their favourite subject: Maths 24, Science 18, English 18. Work out the angle for each subject's sector in a pie chart representing this data.

Mark Scheme — Q2 [6 marks]

  • [1] (a) 180 × (100/360)
  • [1] (a) = 50 students
  • [1] (b) Method: each angle = (frequency/60) × 360
  • [1] (b) Maths: 24/60 × 360 = 144°
  • [1] (b) Science: 18/60 × 360 = 108°
  • [1] (b) English: 18/60 × 360 = 108° (angles sum to 360° ✓)

Mean, Median, Mode & Range from a Frequency Table Grade 7+
Question 4[6 marks]

The table shows the number of goals scored by a football team in 20 matches:

Goals, x01234
Frequency, f36731
(a) [1 mark]
Find the mode.
(b) [2 marks]
Find the median.
(c) [3 marks]
Calculate the mean number of goals scored, to 2 decimal places.

Mark Scheme — Q4 [6 marks]

  • [1] (a) Mode = 2 (highest frequency, 7)
  • [1] (b) Recognises n = 20, median = mean of 10th and 11th values
  • [1] (b) Using cumulative frequencies (3, 9, 16, 19, 20), both 10th and 11th values fall in "2" → Median = 2
  • [1] (c) Σfx = (0×3)+(1×6)+(2×7)+(3×3)+(4×1) = 0+6+14+9+4 = 33
  • [1] (c) Mean = Σfx ÷ Σf = 33 ÷ 20
  • [1] (c) = 1.65
Scatter Graphs
Question 5[5 marks]

A scatter graph compares the age (in years) and value (in £1000s) of 8 used cars. The data shows a clear linear pattern, with a line of best fit: V = 19.5 − 1.8a, where V is value in £1000s and a is age in years.

(a) [1 mark]
Describe the correlation between age and value.
(b) [2 marks]
Use the equation to estimate the value of a car that is 4.5 years old.
(c) [2 marks]
Explain why it would not be reliable to use this equation to estimate the value of a 25-year-old car.

Mark Scheme — Q5 [5 marks]

  • [1] (a) Strong negative correlation — as age increases, value decreases
  • [1] (b) V = 19.5 − 1.8(4.5) = 19.5 − 8.1
  • [1] (b) = £11 400 (11.4 thousand)
  • [1] (c) Recognises this is extrapolation — 25 years is far outside the data range (1–8 years) used to form the line
  • [1] (c) The linear trend may not continue / the equation could even give a meaningless negative value, so the estimate is unreliable
