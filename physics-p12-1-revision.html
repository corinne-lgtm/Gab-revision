💡Big Ideas

Same Speed

All EM waves travel at 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum — the speed of light.

Transverse Waves

EM waves are transverse — electric and magnetic disturbances that transfer energy.

No Matter Needed

EM waves do not transfer matter — only energy, from a source to an absorber.

Energy ∝ Frequency

Higher frequency = shorter wavelength = more energy per photon (gamma rays most energetic).

Core Equation
Wave Speed Equation
v = f × λ
wave speed (m/s) = frequency (Hz) × wavelength (m)
f = v ÷ λto find frequency
λ = v ÷ fto find wavelength

All electromagnetic waves travel at 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s through a vacuum. The speed stays the same — so as frequency increases, wavelength decreases proportionally.

⭐ Exam Tip

Use standard form for large values in calculations. For example, 300 000 000 m/s is written as 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s and 900 000 000 Hz as 9.0 × 10⁸ Hz. Your calculator should have an EXP or ×10ˣ button.

⚖️Compare: Radio vs Gamma

📻 Radio Waves

  • Longest wavelength
  • Lowest frequency
  • Lowest energy
  • Wavelength up to 10⁴ m
  • Used in communications

☢️ Gamma Rays

  • Shortest wavelength
  • Highest frequency
  • Highest energy per photon
  • Wavelength ~10⁻¹² m
  • Emitted by nuclei

⚠️ Don't Confuse

The boundaries of the spectrum are not fixed — different parts blend into each other and the exact dividing lines are approximate. Never write exact boundary values as if they are precise in an exam.
