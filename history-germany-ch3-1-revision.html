3.1 Murders and uprisings

Political violence, extremist threats and challenges to Weimar democracy between 1919–1923.

Weimar faced threats from both the left and the right — making democracy seem weak and unstable.

1923
Red Rising
Communist revolt in Ruhr.

Kapp Putsch

Goal

Replace Weimar with authoritarian government.

Method

Freikorps seized Berlin.

Failure

General strike stopped economy and forced collapse.

Assassinations

Between 1919–1922, over 350 political murders took place. Right-wing extremists killed key Weimar supporters such as Matthias Erzberger and Walter Rathenau.

Why violence mattered

Fear

Democracy looked weak.

Instability

Government constantly threatened.

Polarisation

Society deeply divided.

Extremism

Both sides used force.

Exam summary

Murders and uprisings weakened Weimar because repeated extremist threats made Germany appear unstable and undermined confidence in democracy.
