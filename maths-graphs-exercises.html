Straight Lines and Gradients
Question 1[5 marks]
(a) [3 marks]
Find the equation of the straight line passing through (2, 5) and (4, 11). Give your answer in the form y = mx + c.
(b) [2 marks]
A second line is perpendicular to the line in part (a). Write down its gradient, and explain how you know.

Mark Scheme — Q1 [5 marks]

  • [1] (a) Gradient = (11−5)/(4−2) = 3
  • [1] (a) Substitutes a point to find c: 5 = 3(2) + c → c = −1
  • [1] (a) y = 3x − 1
  • [1] (b) Recognises perpendicular gradients multiply to give −1
  • [1] (b) Gradient = −1/3
Graph Transformations
Question 2[4 marks]

The graph of y = f(x) is transformed to give the graph of y = f(x − 2) + 3. The point (1, 4) lies on y = f(x).

Describe fully the single transformation that maps y = f(x) onto y = f(x−2)+3, and state the new coordinates of the point that was originally (1, 4).

Mark Scheme — Q2 [4 marks]

  • [1] Identifies a translation
  • [1] 2 units in the positive x-direction (right 2)
  • [1] 3 units in the positive y-direction (up 3) — i.e. translation by vector (2, 3)
  • [1] New point: (3, 7)
