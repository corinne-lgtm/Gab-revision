The rise of Hitler and the Nazi dictatorship 40 marks

Answer all six questions. Use the writing tips if you need help. Your answers stay in this browser until you clear its data.

Interpretations A and B

Interpretation A — Adapted from a retrospective account by a German worker: “Hitler promised work and a better future. After years of unemployment, many people were ready to believe him.”

Interpretation B — Adapted from an anti-Nazi observer: “The Nazis used intimidation, propaganda and the fear of communism to increase their support.”

Question 1 (4 marks)

How does Interpretation B differ from Interpretation A about the reasons for Nazi support?

Writing guidanceCompare the main message of each interpretation; quote or paraphrase both.
Indicative mark scheme: A emphasises hope, jobs and economic desperation; B emphasises intimidation, propaganda and fear of communism.

Use this to self-assess; it is not an automatic grade.

Question 1 (4 marks)

Why might the authors of Interpretations A and B have different views?

Writing guidanceUse each author’s position and the historical circumstances; do not simply repeat their words.
Indicative mark scheme: A reflects a worker attracted by the promise of employment during the Depression; B reflects an opponent focusing on Nazi methods and political intimidation.

Use this to self-assess; it is not an automatic grade.

Question 1 (8 marks)

Which interpretation is more convincing about the growth of Nazi support?

Writing guidanceEvaluate BOTH interpretations using precise contextual knowledge, then reach a judgement.
Indicative mark scheme: A is supported by mass unemployment and promises of work and bread. B is supported by Nazi propaganda, SA intimidation and anti-communism. A strong answer weighs their relative importance and reaches a supported judgement.

Use this to self-assess; it is not an automatic grade.

Question 1 (4 marks)

Describe two ways in which Hitler established his dictatorship in 1933.

Writing guidanceGive two distinct ways and develop each with an accurate supporting detail.
Indicative mark scheme: Reichstag Fire Decree of 28 February restricted civil liberties and enabled arrests; Enabling Act of 23 March allowed Hitler to make laws without Reichstag approval.

Use this to self-assess; it is not an automatic grade.

Question 1 (8 marks)

In what ways did Nazi policies affect young people in Germany?

Writing guidanceWrite two developed paragraphs with precise examples and effects.
Indicative mark scheme: Schools promoted Nazi ideology and racial ideas, while the Hitler Youth and League of German Girls organised leisure and taught different gender roles; participation became compulsory in 1939.

Use this to self-assess; it is not an automatic grade.

Question 1 (12 marks)

Which was more important in establishing Nazi control: propaganda or terror?

Writing guidanceExplain BOTH bullet points in developed paragraphs. Weigh them and reach a clear conclusion.
Indicative mark scheme: Propaganda under Goebbels shaped public opinion through radio, rallies and censorship. Terror by the SS, Gestapo and concentration camps discouraged opposition. A reasoned judgement compares how each helped maintain control.

Use this to self-assess; it is not an automatic grade.
