B5.4 Viral diseases

Examples of plant and animal diseases caused by viruses: measles, HIV/AIDS, and tobacco mosaic virus.

📖 Revision ✏️ Questions
Viruses damage and destroy cells — there are no medicines to cure viral diseases. Prevention through vaccination, isolation and hygiene is the key.

HIV/AIDS in detail

🟣

HIV attacks the immune cells. After initial mild illness, the virus remains hidden in the immune system for years — many people don't know they are infected. Gradually the immune system is so damaged that it can no longer deal with other infections or certain cancers — at this point the patient has developed AIDS.

The time between HIV infection and AIDS depends on: level of nutrition, overall health, access to antiretroviral drugs.

💡 Antiretroviral drugs must be started as soon as possible after infection for the best chance of long-term survival. In sub-Saharan Africa, access to these drugs is difficult → life expectancy for HIV+ people is still very low.
